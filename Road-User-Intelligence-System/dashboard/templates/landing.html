Ghana Road Traffic Regulations, L.I. 2180

Precision Safety for School Zones.

A computer-vision system that analyzes real drone footage of a school-zone crossing in Tamale, Ghana — detecting vehicles and pedestrians, estimating vehicle speed, and flagging violations of Ghana's school-zone speed limit and pedestrian right-of-way law.

How the System Works

Detection, tracking, speed estimation, and violation logging, chained into one pipeline.

directions_car

Detection & Tracking

A YOLOv8 model trained on aerial drone imagery detects vehicles and pedestrians frame by frame; ByteTrack assigns each one a persistent identity across the footage.

speed

Speed Estimation

Vehicle motion is mapped to real-world distance using a camera calibration built from measured reference distances at the actual crossing, converting pixel movement into km/h.

assignment_late

Violation Evidence

Vehicles exceeding the legal school-zone speed limit, or failing to yield to a pedestrian on the crossing, are logged with a timestamp, speed reading, and image snapshot.

About This Project

CivicShield is a final-year Computer Science project developed at RUIS, analyzing real fixed-drone footage of a school-zone pedestrian crossing in Tamale, Ghana. It applies Ghana's Road Traffic Act 2004 (Act 683) and Road Traffic Regulations 2012 (L.I. 2180) as the legal basis for its speed-limit and pedestrian-yield violation logic.
