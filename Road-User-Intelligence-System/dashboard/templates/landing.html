Precision Safety for School Zones.

A computer-vision system analyzing real fixed-drone footage of a school-zone crossing in Tamale, Ghana — detecting vehicles and pedestrians, estimating vehicle speed, and flagging violations of Ghana's legal 30km/h speed limit and pedestrian right-of-way law.

How the System Works

Detection, tracking, speed estimation, and violation logging chained into one execution pipeline.

directions_car

Detection & Tracking

A YOLOv8 model trained on aerial drone imagery detects vehicles and pedestrians frame by frame; ByteTrack assigns persistent tracking IDs across footage.

speed

Speed Estimation

Vehicle motion is transformed to metric distance using camera homography calibration built from reference ground points at the crossing, converting pixel displacement to km/h.

assignment_late

Violation Evidence

Vehicles exceeding the 30km/h school-zone limit or failing to yield to pedestrians are logged with timestamps, speed readings, and cropped image snapshots.

About This Project

CivicShield is a final-year Computer Science research project developed at RUIS, analyzing real fixed-drone footage of a school-zone pedestrian crossing in Tamale, Ghana. It applies Ghana's Road Traffic Act 2004 (Act 683) and Road Traffic Regulations 2012 (L.I. 2180) as the legal basis for speed-limit and pedestrian-yield enforcement.
