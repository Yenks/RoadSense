Privacy Policy

How CivicShield collects, uses, and safeguards user account details and traffic monitoring data.

1. Account Information

CivicShield requires user registration via Supabase Authentication. We collect user email addresses strictly for authentication, session verification, and security logging. Passwords are handled through encrypted Supabase Auth protocols and are never logged or stored in plain text.

2. Traffic Safety Analytics & Video Data

The CivicShield system processes fixed aerial drone footage of school zone pedestrian crossings in Tamale, Ghana. Data extracted from video sessions includes vehicle bounding box tracks, calculated speeds (km/h), timestamp logs, and image crop snapshots for flagged traffic violations. Personal identifying details like license plates or face biometrics are not collected or analyzed.

3. Data Access & Sharing

Traffic analysis logs and violation evidence are stored in system databases for academic research and infrastructure evaluation at RUIS. We do not sell, share, or transfer user account data or analytics to third-party commercial entities.

4. Security Measures

Session management utilizes secure HTTP session tokens validated against Supabase Auth. Public facing endpoints are strictly segregated from authenticated analytical reporting routes.
